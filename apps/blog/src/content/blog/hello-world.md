Welcome to the Talasa blog.

This is where we'll write about maritime risk, sanctions screening, and the
engineering behind the platform. Posts are plain Markdown files — drop a new
`.md` into `src/content/blog/` and it ships.

## What to expect

- Short notes on data sources (GLEIF, Equasis, OpenSanctions, GDELT)
- Deeper write-ups on screening methodology
- The occasional engineering log

Thanks for reading.
